Toggle extended thinking mode for deeper analysis.

$ARGUMENTS

Commands:
- `/think` - Toggle thinking mode on/off
- `/think on` - Enable extended thinking
- `/think off` - Disable extended thinking

When enabled, the model uses maximum thinking tokens (128K) for complex reasoning tasks.
